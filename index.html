01 / Understand the risks

Distraction goes beyond your phone.

Our campaign looks at four ways your attention can shift away from driving. One activity can involve more than one.

01 Visual

Eyes off the road.

Looking away from the road can mean missing something ahead.

  • Glancing at a phone screen
  • Turning to look at scenery
  • Looking back at passengers

02 Manual

Hands away from driving.

Other tasks can compete with the hands you need to control your car.

  • Reaching for something in the car
  • Eating or drinking
  • Adjusting the radio or navigation

03 Cognitive

Mind somewhere else.

Attention can drift even when you’re looking straight ahead.

  • Daydreaming
  • Getting absorbed in a conversation
  • Focusing on worries instead of the drive

04 Auditory

Sounds competing for attention.

Competing sounds can make it harder to focus on driving.

  • High-volume music
  • Repeated phone notifications
  • Loud, competing conversations

One action. Several distractions.

Reading or typing a message can occupy your eyes, hands, and thoughts at once. Keep your focus on the drive.
